    03
    Paid Media · Google Ads · Lead Generation

    Google Ads launch strategy for a premium renovation contractor

    MRKTOPS · Digital Marketing Intern · Client: Montreal renovation contractor

    Strategy & setup
    3campaigns planned
    $50/dayCAD budget split across them
    EN + FRkeyword coverage
    Problem

    A Montreal renovation contractor with 40+ years of experience had no Google Ads presence. The goal was high-value leads from affluent homeowners, not high volume.

    What I did
    • Built the launch plan from scratch: account setup and conversion tracking across Google Ads, GA4 and Tag Manager for forms, calls and thank-you page visits
    • Designed a 3-campaign structure (kitchen, bathroom, full home and luxury) with a search-only network and a plan to move from Maximize Clicks to Target CPA once conversion data builds up
    • Targeted affluent West Montreal areas such as Westmount, NDG and Côte-Saint-Luc
    • Researched English and French keywords and built a negative keyword list to filter out low-budget and irrelevant searches
    • Wrote ad copy around trust signals: licensed, insured and 40+ years of experience
    • Specified landing page requirements, including a budget qualifier form to screen leads
    • Set up weekly reporting on spend, clicks, CTR, CPC, leads, cost per lead and search terms
    Deliverable

    A complete, launch-ready strategy and setup plan that gave the client a clear structure for attracting premium leads on a controlled daily budget.
