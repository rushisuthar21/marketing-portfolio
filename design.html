Why it matters for marketing

One action per asset

Date, place and a QR code sit in the same spot on every poster, so people never have to hunt for how to join.

One system, many formats

A poster becomes a square animation without losing its look, which keeps a brand recognisable across channels.

Built with the audience in mind

A casual Q&A gets doodles and sticky notes. A coding fest gets neon green. The style follows who the event is for.
